## Changes from the Altium drawing
**Fixes (the original would not have worked):**
1. **The CC2595 RF_OUT had no DC feed.** Added 12 nH from VDD_PA, non-magnetic (LQW15AN12NJ00D).
2. **The CC2595 RBIAS resistors went to GND.** They now go through 20k to VDD_PA, per the TI EM.
3. **The LT80602 exposed pad is VIN,** so it connects to 5V0. The FB divider was 681k/220k but RFB-BOT can be at most 205k; it is now 634k/200k. FCM mode with a 0.1 µF BST cap.
4. **The 5 V INA219 measured nothing,** because the buck tapped 5V_BUS ahead of the shunt. The buck now runs from 5V0.
5. **The wearable-only MCU nets were removed:** CTRL_SPDT, Mod_Sel, PMIC_Status, Sensor_*, QSPI_CLK.
6. **The CW synth sheet is complete:**
   - TCXO, decoupling, OUTA/OUTAM.
   - Loop filter designed for a 52 MHz PFD and 7.27 mA charge pump: about 59 kHz bandwidth, 62° phase margin.
7. **Top-sheet TODOs:**
   - TX/RX antenna connections: the patch and the edge SMA, per the enclosure.
   - PA GND connected.
   - MCU and synth completed.

**Enclosure-driven changes:**
- **USB-C plug → receptacle,** with CC1 and CC2 Rd. The puck connects by cable.
- **Ferrite parts out of the magnet field** (`docs/magnetics_thermal.md`):
  - The nRF DC/DC inductors are DNP, and REG1 runs in LDO mode (firmware DCDCEN = 0).
  - The three ferrite beads became 1 Ω (PA), 10 Ω (LNA) and 0 Ω (synth) with the existing caps.
  - The buck inductor, crystal and TCXO are kept out of the clocking-bar zone.
- **Patch feed:** runs on B.Cu over a GND island on L3, then through one RF via to L1. Pi-match footprints sit at the patch.
- **SMA launch:** has an L3 GND island under it (L2 is voided in the footprint).

**Other improvements:**
- 50 mΩ shunts.
- LNA on 5V0 and off at reset.
- PA and synth off at reset.
- RX overload pi pad (DNP shunts).
- DNP ESD chokes at both antennas.
- 33 Ω series resistors on the SPI lines.
- USB pair renamed `USB_D+`/`USB_D-`.

## Open items before ordering
- **Patch tuning:**
  - Tune the patch match with a VNA, in the closed enclosure, on a phone. The patch is narrow (about 20–25 MHz at −10 dB), so keep the RX channels at 2430–2455 MHz.
  - Confirm the patch polarisation axis against the TX monopole.
- **Regulatory and exposure:** +20 dBm CW needs frequency hopping (FCC 15.247) and an RF-exposure evaluation at 5 mm.
- **Firmware thermal limits:** throttle TX at 60 °C, turn off at 68 °C, and default to a 25–50 % TX duty cycle.
- **Magnet field check:** measure the field over the board area with a Hall probe. If the ring band reads above 20 mT, keep all ferrites away from it.
- **JLC options:** POFV via-in-pad for the nRF52840 inner balls; ENIG finish; impedance control (0.30/0.20 mm CPWG); confirm the 0.45 mm plated slot.
- **Enclosure:** apply the USB and SMA dimension corrections (`enclosure/CHANGES.md`).
